Short answers to good questions.

Which models can I use?

GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, DeepSeek and more. New models appear as soon as providers release them.

Can I switch models mid-conversation?

Yes. Pick another model and the full thread comes with you, so you never start over.

Do I need my own API keys?

No. Use Manyfold credits, or add your own keys to pay providers directly.

Is my data private?

Your chats are encrypted and never used to train models. Export or delete them whenever you like.
